# Small-Pokemon-go-Trinket-
Want a small little charm to gift to someone? Well this small 3D printable Pokemon GO Style gift is perfect. Its a super simple trinket that you can print for yourself or gift to someone else. All of the files are linked below and it just needs to be 3D printed and most likely painted. 

I decided to make this project as a small little charm that would be easily 3D printable and could be gifted to people I meet. I thought about how much fun it was to play Pokemon GO as a kid with friends and how cool it was to see these little gift packages fill my screen. I think it would be cool to be able to bottle up that feeling and share it with others in the real world!
